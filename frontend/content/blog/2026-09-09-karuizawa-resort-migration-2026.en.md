## 2. Evolving Lifestyles: From Summer Villas to Year-Round Residences

The biggest paradigm shift in Karuizawa is the transition to "year-round" usage. Previously, Karuizawa was firmly established in people's minds as a summer retreat. Today, however, highly airtight and well-insulated homes capable of overcoming severe winter cold, along with new builds and renovated properties featuring whole-house floor heating and wood-burning stoves, are becoming the standard. As a result, "winter life in Karuizawa"—where one can stay warm and comfortable while enjoying snowy landscapes—has successfully taken root.

Furthermore, this shift toward permanent residency is fundamentally supported by the development of advanced digital infrastructure. With an increasing number of migrants moving with remote work in mind, Karuizawa Town itself is strongly promoting digital transformation (DX) in its administration. For instance, in September 2025, the Karuizawa Town Office introduced cloud PBX systems and smartphones for all staff, consolidating major communication equipment into robust external data centers. This has built a resilient infrastructure (BCP measures) ensuring communication networks remain intact even in the event of a disaster.

Such municipal-level infrastructure resilience leveraging data centers, combined with the assurance of stable, high-speed communication environments, gives business executives and IT professionals the peace of mind that they can "work with the exact same speed and efficiency as in Tokyo." Conducting cutting-edge business while escaping the hustle and bustle of the city and enjoying the lush, forested nature right outside the window—this new lifestyle is powerfully boosting the demand for permanent residency in Karuizawa.

## 3. Remote Work and Excellent Educational Environments Fuel Relocation Demand Among Families

A major characteristic of today's Karuizawa market is the rapid increase in primary home purchases not only by the wealthy but also by families in their 30s and 40s raising children. Behind this is a boom in "educational relocation," spurred by the establishment of educational institutions with unique teaching philosophies. Inquiry-based learning facilities, most notably Karuizawa Kazakoshi Gakuen (軽井沢風越学園), have garnered nationwide attention, earning strong support from parents who wish to raise their children freely in a natural environment.

Looking at actual environmental data, demand for family-oriented housing is also steadily rising in the school districts of public schools such as Karuizawa Tobu Elementary School (軽井沢東部小学校) and Karuizawa Junior High School (軽井沢中学校). These schools provide education that leverages the local nature and rich international culture, and many families enjoy Karuizawa's unique environment while sending their children to public schools.

Furthermore, a crucial latest trend in 2026 is the generous child-rearing support system provided by Karuizawa Town. Starting in August 2026, medical expenses for children up to 18 years old will be completely free, highlighting a town-wide effort to strengthen support for families. High educational standards, flexible working styles enabled by remote work, and robust municipal backing—these three elements have combined to redefine Karuizawa's value from "a place to spend the weekend" to "a place to raise children and establish a family's home base."

## 5. Why Do High-End Resort Properties Retain Their Asset Value?

The reason high-end properties in Karuizawa resist depreciation lies in the preservation of landscapes and environments through strict "building regulations."

As actual data shows, many areas in Karuizawa are designated as a "Category I Exclusively Low-Story Residential Zone (第１種低層住居専用地域)" or a "City Planning Area outside of Urbanization Promotion and Urbanization Control Areas (市街化区域及び市街化調整区域外の都市計画区域)." For example, parts of the Oiwake area in the acquired data are subject to extremely strict limitations: a "30% building coverage ratio and 50% floor area ratio." Because of this, it is impossible to build across an entire plot. Ample spacing between neighboring houses is maintained, preventing reckless development and guaranteeing a spacious, beautiful forest landscape well into the future.

Furthermore, the completion of a large-scale redevelopment project in front of the station serves as a clear demand driver pushing up asset values.

- March 2024: Construction began on a new redevelopment utilizing the former tracks of the Former Shin-Etsu Main Line (旧信越本線) (approx. 13,000 sq m).
- February 2026: Completion of the facility complex.
- March 17, 2026: Grand opening of the new multi-purpose commercial facility, Karuizawa T-SITE (軽井沢 T-SITE).

Spearheaded by companies like Mitsubishi Estate (三菱地所), this redevelopment created a low-rise, decentralized facility comprising six steel-framed buildings (single-story, two-story, and partially three-story) in a prime location directly connected to the station. Under the concept of "broadcasting and experiencing the essence of Shinshu (Nagano)," the complex hosts 17 establishments, including hot spring facilities, accommodations, a share lounge, dining, and retail. With the area around Karuizawa Station evolving from a mere "transit point" to a "destination hub," the convenience and brand value of the surrounding areas have been elevated to yet another level.

## 6. A Perfect Balance of Nature and Urban Functions! The Reality of Livability and the Living Environment

When considering relocation or permanent residency, everyday "livability" is the top concern. Karuizawa is a rare area that boasts an overwhelming natural environment while offering living infrastructure on par with urban centers.

Karuizawa Station, the core of transportation access, is operated by the East Japan Railway Company (東日本旅客鉄道 - JR East) and handles 5,436 passengers daily. Using the Hokuriku Shinkansen, it takes only about an hour to reach Tokyo Station (東京駅)—a stress-free distance for hybrid workers who commute to the city center a few times a week.

The medical environment is also well-equipped. According to the acquired data, at least three medical institutions have been confirmed around the target area, including Kitazawa Clinic (北沢クリニック), Karuizawa Arai Dental Clinic (軽井沢あらい歯科), and Karuizawa Orthodontic Dental Clinic (軽井沢矯正歯科). Routine colds, pediatric care, and dental treatments can be smoothly handled within the town, and in the unlikely event that advanced medical care is needed, there is a backup system featuring large-scale core hospitals like Saku Central Hospital (佐久総合病院), located just a 30 to 40-minute drive away.

When it comes to shopping, large supermarkets offering fresh local ingredients (such as Tsuruya Karuizawa Store / ツルヤ軽井沢店) and commercial facilities like the Prince Shopping Plaza (プリンスショッピングプラザ) are within a radius suitable for daily use, achieving a perfect balance between abundant nature and urban functionality.

## 7. Crucial Points and Caveats Every Primary Homebuyer Should Know When Selecting a Property

On the other hand, when choosing real estate in Karuizawa for a primary home or investment, there is one caveat you must never overlook: checking the "natural disaster risk (hazards)."

Because Karuizawa is surrounded by rich water systems and deep forests, risks of flooding and landslides exist depending on the area. According to the latest hazard data from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (国土地理院) acquired by Mekiki Research, the following risk assessments were found around the search locations:

- Flood Risk: Present (Maximum inundation depth Rank 4: estimated inundation of 5 to 10 meters)
- Landslide Risk: None (No designated phenomena around the location)

As shown, even in places with low landslide danger, there are real areas where flood risks (inundation depths of 5–10 meters) due to river overflows are anticipated. Before purchasing, it is essential to check the hazard map and hedge your risks—either by ensuring architectural measures like raised foundations are in place or by choosing a safe, elevated area.

Additionally, if the zoning is designated as a "Category I Exclusively Low-Story Residential Zone," operations such as a combined shop and residence may be restricted. Verifying legal regulations in line with your future life plans is also crucial.
