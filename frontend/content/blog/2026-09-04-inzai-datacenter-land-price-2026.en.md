## 1. Why Inzai City, Chiba? The Background of a Global Data Center Hub

In recent years, global mega-tech companies such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google, and Microsoft have unanimously chosen Inzai City, Chiba Prefecture as their data center base in Japan. The biggest reasons for this are the area's overwhelming "ground stability" and "low disaster risk."

The geographical advantage of being close to the coastal areas of Chiba Prefecture, where submarine cable landing stations are located, minimizes communication latency and has propelled Inzai City into a global data center hub.

## 3. Exponential Infrastructure Development Driven by Data Center Concentration

The concentration of data centers does more than just dot the landscape with massive facilities; it dramatically upgrades the infrastructure environment of the surrounding area. It is said that a single giant data center consumes as much electricity as an entire regional city.

Therefore, infrastructure companies like Tokyo Electric Power Company (東京電力, TEPCO) are rapidly constructing new extra-high voltage substations and laying high-capacity transmission lines around Inzai City. Regarding telecommunications infrastructure, the multiplexing of dark fiber (unused optical fiber lines) and the advanced deployment of 5G/6G base stations are underway, building one of the top-tier communication environments in Japan.

The establishment of these "thick pipelines of power and communication" serves as a powerful fundamental factor that fundamentally supports real estate asset values. While the direct benefits to standard residential housing might be limited, the potential as industrial land has grown immeasurably. This is the driving force behind the rising land prices of surrounding plots, which will be discussed later.
