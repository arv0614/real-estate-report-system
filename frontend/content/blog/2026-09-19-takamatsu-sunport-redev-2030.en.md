## 1. What is the Large-Scale Redevelopment of Sunport Takamatsu Leading Up to 2030?

Currently, the biggest factor driving the real estate market in Takamatsu City is the massive Sunport Takamatsu redevelopment project. In this beautiful waterfront area adjacent to Takamatsu Port and JR Takamatsu Station, a massive public-private development is underway, rapidly advancing the sophistication of urban functions.

Let's break down the future projections and evolutionary steps of the target area in a timeline format.

- March 2024: TAKAMATSU ORNE (タカマツ オルネ), a station building directly connected to JR Takamatsu Station, opened. Featuring a supermarket and restaurants making their first appearance in Shikoku, the commercial functions around the station have been significantly expanded.
- February 2025: Anabuki Arena Kagawa / Kagawa Prefectural Arena (あなぶきアリーナ香川 / 香川県立アリーナ), one of the largest multi-purpose arenas in the Chugoku and Shikoku regions, opened its doors. It is creating a new influx of visitors through national sports tournaments and concerts.
- April 2025: The Kagawa Campus of Tokushima Bunri University (徳島文理大学) fully relocated to the Sunport area. It has been reborn as a vibrant educational district where thousands of students and faculty members come and go daily.
- Currently in 2026: With the new arena and station building fully operational, the city is in the midst of a growth phase where daily bustle and economic ripple effects are driving up surrounding real estate demand.
- Summer 2027 and beyond: Mandarin Oriental Setouchi, Takamatsu (マンダリン オリエンタル 瀬戸内 高松), the first international luxury hotel brand to enter Shikoku, is scheduled to open. It will serve as an international tourism hub targeting affluent travelers from around the world.
- 2030: Designated as a "Decarbonization Leading Area" by the Ministry of the Environment (環境省), the Sunport Takamatsu area will see the widespread use of renewable energy and EV mobility, completing a sustainable, next-generation urban model.

In this way, Takamatsu City is dynamically transforming beyond the framework of a mere regional core city into an international hub for tourism, business, and education. Looking ahead to 2030, the data clearly shows that real estate values and residential demand in the surrounding areas will only continue to rise.

Opened in 2025, Anabuki Arena Kagawa was designed by the world-renowned architectural firm SANAA. It is one of the largest arenas in the Chugoku and Shikoku regions, capable of accommodating up to approximately 10,000 people. With national sports competitions and arena tours by famous artists now held frequently, its ability to attract visitors from outside the prefecture has dramatically improved. This has not only brought economic benefits to surrounding accommodations and restaurants but has also clearly manifested positive impacts on real estate investments, such as increased demand for short-term vacation rentals and rising commercial tenant rents.

Furthermore, Mandarin Oriental Setouchi, Takamatsu, slated to open in the summer of 2027, is an ultra-luxury hotel where room rates are rumored to be around 100,000 yen per night. These zoning regulations are more than sufficient for the development of high-rise condominiums and large mixed-use buildings, serving as a strong foundation to attract even more private investment in the future.

While it is easy to be captivated by the glamorous aspects of the redevelopment, the most important point for end-user homebuyers, which they will experience every day, is "daily livability." In this regard, Takamatsu City boasts an exceptionally excellent infrastructure environment.

## 7. Key Points for Choosing a Property Without Failing, from the Perspectives of Both End-Users and Investors

Finally, we will explain the "risk management" that must absolutely be checked when purchasing real estate in Takamatsu City. No matter how much redevelopment increases the city's appeal and expected economic benefits, it is all for nothing if you overlook the risk of natural disasters.

According to hazard information from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (国土地理院) included in our survey data, while there is "no risk of sediment disasters" around the target area in Takamatsu City, it is clearly determined that there is a "risk of flooding." In particular, maximum caution is required as the area includes zones where the maximum inundation depth rank is estimated at "4 (5–10 meters)."

If you are building a home (detached house) for personal use, architectural ingenuity is essential, such as implementing physical anti-flooding measures like raising the foundation higher than usual, or placing the living room and bedrooms on the second floor or higher just in case. Also, even when purchasing a condominium for investment, it is necessary to firmly confirm in advance whether critical facilities like electrical rooms and pump rooms are located underground or on the first floor (to prevent the entire building's infrastructure from failing due to flooding).

In choosing real estate, returns (value appreciation due to redevelopment) and risks (natural disasters) are always two sides of the same coin.
