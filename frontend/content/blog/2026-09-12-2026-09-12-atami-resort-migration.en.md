Boasting overwhelmingly convenient access—just a 40-minute bullet train ride from central Tokyo—this city has undergone a dramatic evolution. Once known primarily as a "hot spring tourist destination" catering to large group tours, it has transformed into a "resort for living." Today, it is a magnet for wealthy individuals seeking relocation or second homes, as well as an ideal candidate for families looking for their primary residence.

## 1. Introduction: In 2026, Atami is Hot Again! The Background of Wealthy Influx and Resurgent Land Prices

When analyzing the current real estate market in Atami, the most notable trend is the polarization between "skyrocketing prices of premium properties driven by the influx of the wealthy" and "steady demand from primary homebuyers." Let's first grasp the overall market picture through actual transaction data.

According to the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism real estate transaction data covering the period from 2021 to 2025 (sample size: 2,015 transactions), the average transaction price in Atami is approximately 23.54 million yen, with a median of 12 million yen. The average unit price stands at around 57,000 yen per square meter.

At first glance, these market prices may seem affordable, but the price range is what truly stands out. While there is an extreme case of a minimum transaction price of just 200 yen (presumably forest land or plots without road access), the maximum transaction price reaches an astonishing 1.8 billion yen. This is solid evidence of active purchasing of large-scale villas by affluent individuals and the acquisition of land for resort development by businesses.

## 2. Just 40 Minutes from Central Tokyo: How Bullet Train Commuting is Shaping the "Resort for Living"

Without a doubt, Atami's greatest strength is its accessibility. Using the Tokaido Shinkansen (東海道新幹線), you can reach Tokyo Station (東京駅) in about 40 to 50 minutes. With direct access to Shinagawa Station (品川駅) as well, Atami functions perfectly as a "commutable area" for hybrid workers who commute to central Tokyo a few times a week.

Additionally, Kinomiya Station (来宮駅), operated by the East Japan Railway Company (東日本旅客鉄道) and located just one stop from Atami Station, is an essential area when discussing livability. According to actual data, Kinomiya Station handles 2,274 passengers a day. Unlike the tourist-packed area around Atami Station, the vicinity of Kinomiya Station features quiet residential neighborhoods. It is highly popular among primary homebuyers for its excellent everyday shopping options and convenient living environment.

Looking at actual transaction data, there are properties in price ranges highly attractive to singles and DINKs. For example, in the Izusan (伊豆山) area, the following second-hand condo transactions have been recorded:
- 1LDK (1-bedroom) (60 sq.m., built in 1962): Approx. 4.9 million yen
- 2LDK (2-bedroom) (85 sq.m., built in 1993): Approx. 22 million yen

With older properties, it is entirely feasible to acquire an ocean-view condo for under 5 million yen, renovate it to your liking, and fully enjoy a dual-city lifestyle.

## 4. Not Just for Investors: Why Families and Singles are Drawn to Atami's Livability

Beyond its identity as a resort destination, Atami actually has a very solid foundation as a "city for living." The abundance of medical facilities and educational environments, in particular, serves as a major source of reassurance for families and retirees deciding to relocate.

In our recent actual data extraction, we identified as many as 25 medical institutions just around the designated area. Specifically, there are numerous community-based clinics dotted throughout, such as Aoki Clinic (アオキ クリニック), Dobashi Urology Clinic (どばし泌尿器科クリニック), and Kasai Clinic (河西クリニック), ensuring that residents will never struggle with everyday illnesses or regular medical visits.

Regarding the educational environment, access to public schools like Daiichi Elementary School (第一小学校) and Atami Junior High School (熱海中学校) is well-secured. For families wanting to step away from the overcrowded educational environment of central Tokyo and raise their children freely amidst the ocean and mountains, Atami can be described as a city that perfectly balances "nature" and "urban infrastructure."

## 5. Hot Springs, Nature, and Food: An Upgraded Everyday Life and Child-Rearing Environment

The greatest advantage of living in Atami is that the way you spend your days off becomes dramatically richer. Savoring fresh seafood, soaking in famous hot springs on a day trip, and taking a stroll along the Sun Beach—the essence of the Atami lifestyle is that the "extraordinary" becomes your "ordinary."

Furthermore, Atami City has recently been putting immense effort into welcoming new residents and supporting child-rearing. According to the latest municipal information (as of 2026), generous support systems like the following are available:

- Relocation and Employment Support Subsidy: Subsidies are provided to those relocating from the Tokyo metropolitan area who meet certain conditions. Notably, when relocating with children under 18, an additional 1 million yen is granted per child (applications accepted in 2026 as well), serving as a powerful incentive for families.
- Newlywed Life Support Subsidy: Couples where both partners are 29 or younger can receive up to 600,000 yen, and those 39 or younger up to 300,000 yen, to help cover housing and moving expenses.
- Child-Rearing Support: A seamless support system from pregnancy through the child-rearing years has been established, including the Comprehensive Support Center for Families with Children "Isshoni-ne!" (子育て世代包括支援センターいっしょにね！), alongside a comprehensive medical expense subsidy system for children.

It is precisely because of this financial and emotional support that Atami is beginning to be chosen not just as a "villa destination" but as a "place for permanent residence."

## 7. Future Challenges and Prospects: Infrastructure and the Integration of Old and New Resident Communities

While the real estate market in Atami is full of bright prospects, there are risks and challenges that must be checked before purchasing. The most prominent of these is "natural disaster risk (hazards)."

According to the hazard information within the actual data we retrieved this time, the following clear risks are indicated for the relevant areas in Atami:
- Flood Risk: Present. Maximum inundation depth rank 4 (5 to 10 meters)
- Landslide Risk: Present (danger of debris flows and steep slope collapses)

Because Atami has a bowl-shaped terrain where the ocean and mountains closely meet, it lives side-by-side with the risk of landslides and river flooding during heavy rains as the price for its scenic landscapes. Especially when considering hilltop ocean-view properties or land along mountain streams, it is imperative to check the latest hazard maps and have experts assess retaining wall strength and evacuation routes.
Areas with scarce data or extremely cheap land (such as the aforementioned 300,000 yen land in Izusan) often harbor such topographical and disaster-related risks, making comparisons with neighborhood market prices and on-site inspections absolutely indispensable.

Additionally, with the rapid increase in new residents, integrating the community of long-time locals with newcomers will become a regional challenge moving forward. Participating in neighborhood associations and showing respect for local rules (such as garbage disposal and hot spring usage regulations) are essential prerequisites for enjoying a rich life in Atami.
