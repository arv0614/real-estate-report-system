## 1. The Underlying Strength of Regional Core Cities! Matsuyama City's Redevelopment Potential for 2030

### Redevelopment Overview
Matsuyama City is the capital of Ehime Prefecture and a regional core city with the largest population in Shikoku. In recent years, the redevelopment of regional cities has garnered nationwide attention, and among them, the "Matsuyama Station Surrounding Land Readjustment Project" (松山駅周辺土地区画整理事業) is positioned as a massive project that will elevate the city's potential to the next level.

When considering real estate investment or purchasing a home, it is crucial to grasp the city's future vision in chronological order. Here, we have summarized the future forecast of the target area in a timeline format.

* Autumn 2024: Completion of the JR Matsuyama Station elevation; opening of the new station building and the under-viaduct commercial facility "Dandan Dori" (だんだん通り).
* As of 2026: Land readjustment projects around the station are in full swing; private investments accelerate, including the construction of large-scale condominiums at the West Exit.
* Spring 2027: Temporary use of the East Exit station plaza begins (featuring parking for up to 80 vehicles, event spaces, artificial turf lounging areas, etc.).
* 2030–2031: Scheduled completion of a full-fledged bustling hub, including the transportation terminal "Busta Matsuyama" (バスタ松山).

As shown, the Matsuyama area is steadily updating its urban functions over the span of a few years, making it a highly attractive growth market even from a long-term perspective.

## 2. The Full Picture of the JR Matsuyama Station Elevation Project: Unifying the City and Enhancing Safety

For many years, the urban area around JR Matsuyama Station was divided into east and west by railway tracks, causing chronic traffic congestion and the risk of railroad crossing accidents. However, with the completion of the approximately 2.4 km elevation project of the Yosan Line (予讃線), these issues have been fundamentally resolved.

The elimination of railroad crossings has smoothed the flow of cars and pedestrians. This is a massive benefit, especially for families with small children, as the safety of school commute routes has dramatically improved.

Furthermore, the improved east-west access has led to a re-evaluation of real estate values in the West Exit area, which had previously been perceived as the "backside" of the station. In fact, the construction of large condominium complexes such as "Clair Homes Matsuyama Station J.CREST Tower" (クレアホームズ松山駅 J.CRESTタワー) is underway around the West Exit, rapidly boosting its brand value as a new residential area.

## 3. The Birth of the Station Plaza and New Commercial Facilities: How Will Daily Shopping and Weekends Change?

Redevelopment around the station directly impacts residents' quality of life (QOL). The under-viaduct commercial facility "Dandan Dori," which opened alongside the new station building, brings together restaurants, souvenir shops, and stores convenient for daily shopping, making casual outings after work or on weekends vastly more enjoyable.

Moreover, the evolution of the East Exit area is particularly noteworthy. In preparation for the full-scale development of the "Busta Matsuyama" bus terminal in fiscal 2031, a temporary plaza utilizing the site of the former station building will open in spring 2027. Planned to feature artificial turf lounging spaces and bike-sharing hubs, it will likely become a new community space bustling with families on weekends.

Transforming from a mere "transit point" to a "destination to stay and enjoy," the area around Matsuyama Station is developing an impeccable living environment for those looking to build a home.

## 6. Land Price Trends Real Estate Investors Should Watch: Rental Demand and Future Forecasts for the Matsuyama Station Area

### Impact on Land Prices
We will analyze the reality of Matsuyama City's real estate market based on objective data. According to transaction data from 2021 to 2025 (sample size: 6,940 cases), the average transaction price across Matsuyama City is approximately 24.5 million yen, the median is 16 million yen, and the average unit price is about 80,000 yen/sqm. The gap between the average and the median suggests that while some commercial land in front of the station and high-end properties are pulling the average up, houses and land for actual residential use remain in a relatively affordable price range.

Let's look at specific sample transactions. In the quiet residential areas of "Ishite Shiraishi" (石手白石) and "Imazaike" (今在家), there is a record of a wooden residential property (land and building) built in a Category I Exclusively Low-Story Residential Zone (50% building coverage ratio, 80% floor area ratio) transacting for about 27 million yen. On the other hand, in the more urban "Iwasakicho" (岩崎町) area, an RC (reinforced concrete) apartment building located in a Category I Exclusively Medium-High Residential Zone (60% building coverage ratio, 200% floor area ratio) was sold for 29 million yen. This clearly shows a gradient in prices depending on zoning and structural types.

## 7. Property Selection from a Homebuyer's Perspective: Key Points for Preserving Asset Value

To choose a home where you can live for a long time while preserving its future asset value, checking "safety" (natural disaster risks) is just as essential as convenience.

According to hazard data from Mekiki Research, no landslide disaster risks (土砂災害現象) have been confirmed around the target area in Matsuyama City. However, regarding floods, the area is assessed to have a risk of a "maximum inundation depth of 5 to 10 meters (Rank 4)."

It is crucial to take this fact seriously and always check the hazard map when selecting a property. Countermeasures are indispensable, such as choosing a middle floor or higher if it's a condominium, designing a higher foundation if it's a detached house, or confirming evacuation routes in advance. Accurately grasping the risks and selecting a location with those factors factored in directly ties into the long-term preservation of your asset value and the protection of your family's lives.
