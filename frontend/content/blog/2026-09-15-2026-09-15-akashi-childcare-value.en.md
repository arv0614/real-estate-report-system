When the population increases, the need for housing naturally rises. This "population influx driven by child-rearing support" serves as a powerful engine boosting the real estate value of Akashi City.

## 2. A Must-See for Families! Akashi's Unique "Child-rearing Support Measures" Including Free Medical Care

When talking about Akashi City, you absolutely cannot overlook its unique child-rearing support measures known as the "Seven Free (7つの無料化)." The most significant point is that there are "no income restrictions for parents" across all these policies. The system, which ensures that all children living in Akashi City receive equal support regardless of household income, is highly supported by a wide range of demographics, from dual-income power couples to average families.

Specifically, the following measures are being implemented:
*   Free Medical Care for Children: Medical expenses covered by insurance for both outpatient visits and hospitalizations are completely free for children from age 0 to 18 (high school graduation).
*   Free Childcare Fees: Childcare fees are completely free for the second child onwards (ages 0-2 classes). For classes for ages 3-5, it is free for all households.
*   Free School Lunches: In addition to free junior high school lunches, elementary school lunch fees will also have zero parental burden starting in February 2026.
*   Regular Diaper Delivery: From 3 months to 1 year of age, baby products such as diapers are delivered free of charge to the home every month (also serving as a welfare check visit).

It is said that these measures reduce the monthly financial burden on child-rearing households by tens of thousands of yen. For prospective homebuyers, the fact that this "saved money" can be redirected to mortgage payments, future educational expenses, or family leisure activities serves as a strong push to buy a house in Akashi City. Furthermore, in 2026, a "Child-rearing Support Allowance" will be provided to cope with inflation, showcasing the local government's agility, which in turn brings a sense of security to the residents.

## 3. Livability Around Akashi Station: A Living Environment Where Commercial Facilities Harmonize with the Nature of the Sea and Mountains

The appeal of Akashi City goes beyond its institutional systems. The environment of the city itself, which serves as the stage for daily life, holds extremely high potential. Particularly around Akashi Station, the central hub, the area beautifully harmonizes urban convenience with abundant nature.

Large-scale redevelopment in the south district in front of Akashi Station has been completed, and it now displays a mature vibrancy. The landmark multi-purpose commercial facility in front of the station, "Papios Akashi (パピオスあかし)," houses not only supermarkets and restaurants but also city hall counters, a library, and even an indoor child support facility called the "Akashi Children's Plaza (あかしこども広場)." Having an environment where children can play freely even on rainy days is incredibly reassuring for families. Additionally, the west building of "piole Akashi (ピオレ明石)" inside the station was renovated and reopened in the spring of 2025, further enhancing shopping convenience.

A short walk from the station to the north leads to "Akashi Park (明石公園)," which boasts vast greenery and bustles with families enjoying picnics on weekends. On the south side, there is the lively "Uonotana Shopping Street (魚の棚商店街)" lined with fresh seafood, and further ahead lies the peaceful seaside scenery of the "Akashi Strait (明石海峡)." A compact and livable environment where shopping, administrative services, and interaction with nature can all be completed within walking distance is the greatest strength of the area around Akashi Station.

## 4. A Living Environment That Resonates with Homebuyers: Excellent Commuting Access to Kobe and Osaka, and Weekend Spots

When discussing Akashi City as a commuter town, one cannot overlook its overwhelmingly excellent transportation access. Using the Special Rapid Service (新快速) on the JR Kobe Line (JR神戸線), it takes only about 15 minutes from Akashi Station to Sannomiya Station (三ノ宮駅), the heart of Kobe, and about 40 minutes to the massive terminal of Osaka Station (大阪駅).

For modern families where dual incomes are the norm, reducing commuting time directly translates to an improvement in "time performance (tai-pa)." You can smoothly commute to the city center on weekdays and return to your local town where your children are waiting right after work. This exquisite distance is highly evaluated by households aiming to balance work and child-rearing.

In addition, since the Shinkansen stops at the adjacent Nishi-Akashi Station, it offers outstanding convenience for business trips, visiting hometowns, and traveling across Japan. On weekends, you can not only relax within Akashi City but also easily take a quick car or train ride to nearby large commercial facilities like "Mitsui Outlet Park (三井アウトレットパーク)" in Kobe. This provides an environment where it's easy to switch between work and leisure. For home buyers who prioritize quality of life (QOL), Akashi City can truly be called an ideal living environment.

As the redevelopment around Akashi Station settles and enters a mature phase, the new driving force for development in Akashi City is the area around Nishi-Akashi Station. In June 2026, a new ticket gate and the new station building "PLICO Nishi-Akashi South Building" celebrated their grand opening at the south exit of Nishi-Akashi Station. This station building, which houses tenants like "Matsumoto Kiyoshi (マツモトキヨシ)," "Komeda's Coffee (コメダ珈琲店)," and medical clinics, has dramatically improved the daily convenience of local residents. Because there are so many people who "absolutely want to live in this city," the demand for land for apartments and detached houses constantly exceeds the supply.
