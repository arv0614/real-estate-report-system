## 4. More Than Just an Investment! Why Onna Village is Chosen for Homes and Relocation

While Onna Village's real estate garners high attention from investors, it is also strongly supported by genuine homebuyers and families relocating from cities to raise children. One major reason for this is the existence of generous housing and relocation support systems provided by the local government.

To promote permanent residency, Onna Village offers its own unique subsidy programs. Here are some representative examples:

*   Onna Village Three-Generation Co-residence and Proximity Living Support Program (恩納村三世代同近居支援事業)
    This system subsidizes housing acquisition and moving costs for parents, children, and grandchildren to live together (co-residence or living nearby) in Onna Village. When relocators from outside the village build or purchase a new home for co-residence, they can receive a subsidy of up to 1 million JPY. Even for proximity living, 800,000 JPY is provided, plus an additional 100,000 JPY for moving expenses from outside the prefecture. This serves as a highly attractive incentive for those who want to relocate while valuing family bonds.
*   Onna Village Vacant House Utilization Subsidy (恩納村空家活用事業補助金)
    This system subsidizes renovation costs for relocators and others who renovate and live in vacant houses within the village. The subsidy covers 50% of eligible expenses, up to a maximum of 2 million JPY. For relocators who want to buy old traditional houses or used properties cheaply and renovate them to their liking, this provides powerful support that significantly reduces initial costs.

It is precisely because of these substantial financial benefits that Onna Village continues to be chosen not just as an "aspirational resort," but as a "realistic relocation destination."

## 5. Livability in Harmony with Nature: The Reality of Daily Shopping and Living Infrastructure

Living in Onna Village means being surrounded by rich nature, but when relocating or purchasing real estate, it is essential to understand the "reality of living infrastructure" based on objective data.

First, from a safety perspective, the data is very positive. According to hazard data from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (国土地理院), the area around this survey point is judged to have "no risk" for both floods and landslides. Despite being a resort area close to mountains and the sea, the existence of areas with low flood risk is a major reassurance from an asset protection standpoint. However, as is true for all of Okinawa Prefecture, the risks of strong winds and salt damage from typhoons are ever-present. Before purchasing, it is crucial to check the latest hazard maps and choose building materials treated for wind and salt resistance.

On the other hand, daily living infrastructure presents challenges unique to resort areas. Environmental data shows that there are no railway stations and "0" medical institutions (within the scope of this survey) in the village. In other words, life in Onna Village requires accepting a "completely car-dependent society."
While minor colds or routine check-ups might be handled by small local clinics, seeing a specialist or visiting a general hospital requires a 30-minute to 1-hour drive to base hospitals in neighboring cities like Nago City (名護市), Okinawa City (沖縄市), or Uruma City (うるま市). Daily shopping also heavily relies on large supermarkets and shopping malls in neighboring municipalities, making owning two or more cars the standard for families.

While there may be some inconveniences regarding living infrastructure, the "excellent childcare environment" more than makes up for it. Onna Village puts significant effort into education and childcare support, creating a foundation where families can settle with peace of mind.

As confirmed by the data, public schools such as Onna Elementary School (恩納小学校) and Unna Junior High School (うんな中学校) are established within the village. Unique educational programs that allow children to learn freely in an environment surrounded by the sea and forests provide precious experiences that can never be gained in a concrete urban jungle.

Furthermore, the local government's unique financial childcare support is extensive.
For example, in fiscal year 2025 (2025–2026), a "Cost-of-Living Childcare Support Allowance (物価高対応子育て応援手当)" is being implemented, providing 20,000 JPY per eligible child. Additionally, they have introduced the "Onna Village Universal Childcare Attendance System (Infant Childcare Support Program) (恩納村こども誰でも通園制度（乳児通園支援事業）)" ahead of national programs, advancing the development of flexible childcare environments that support dual-income and nuclear families.
The beautiful natural environment coupled with this meticulous municipal support system serves as a dual engine, elevating Onna Village's reputation among families as the "best place to raise children."

## 7. Key Points and Precautions When Choosing an Area for Relocation or Dual Residency

Finally, we will explain the legal regulations and precautions for choosing an area when purchasing real estate in Onna Village from an expert's perspective.

Looking at actual data, it becomes clear that many transaction samples in Onna Village fall "Outside City Planning Areas (都市計画区域外)." Consequently, data regarding the building coverage ratio (the ratio of the building area to the site area) and floor area ratio (the ratio of the total floor area to the site area) is listed as "null (unspecified)."
At first glance, this might seem like a benefit, allowing you to "freely build a house of any size you like." Indeed, it is the perfect environment for building a single-story villa taking advantage of a vast plot or a spacious garage house.

However, being outside a city planning area means it includes zones where "the provision of public infrastructure like water/sewage and paved roads is not guaranteed by the local government."
Even if you acquire the land cheaply, it is not uncommon to face situations when building a house such as, "bringing in water pipes cost millions of yen out of pocket," "installing a septic tank was mandatory," or "disputes arose over access rights to a private road."
Therefore, when looking for land in Onna Village, making sure to check the current status of electricity, water/sewage, and telecommunications infrastructure through a professional lens—not just looking at price and views—is an absolute requirement for success. Since some areas have sparse public transaction data, it is essential to compare not just surface-level market prices, but the "total project cost," including infrastructure development expenses, with neighboring market rates.
