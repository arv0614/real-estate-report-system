## 2. The Background of the Population Boom: Progressive Childcare Support Measures Attracting Families

The rapid population growth in Shingu Town, particularly its high ratio of children (20.7%), is supported by strong, municipality-led "childcare support policies." Researching the latest trends in 2026 reveals clear reasons why Shingu Town is the top choice for families.

First is the depth of its financial support. In Shingu Town, medical expenses for children are "free (for both outpatient and inpatient care) with no income restrictions until the end of the fiscal year when the child turns 18 (high school age)." Covering medical costs up to the high school years—when educational expenses typically increase—provides immeasurable financial relief to households.

Second is the robust support for dual-income households. In addition to achieving zero children on daycare waiting lists, the town has fully implemented the "Universal Childcare Access System" (こども誰でも通園制度) in 2026. This creates an environment where parents can flexibly use childcare facilities regardless of their employment status. Furthermore, the Shingu Town Children and Families Center "Hug-uru" (はぐうる) functions as a comprehensive support hub, establishing a seamless consultation system from pregnancy through the child-rearing years.

Looking at the actual data, educational environments such as Shingu Elementary School (新宮小学校) and Shingu Junior High School (新宮中学校) are well established, reflecting the community's high interest in educational standards. Additionally, when purchasing a home, buyers can combine housing subsidies such as Fukuoka Prefecture's "Children's Renovation Subsidy" (こどもリノベ補助金) and the national "Housing Energy Efficiency 2026 Campaign" (住宅省エネ2026キャンペーン) to significantly reduce the costs of relocating and settling in Shingu Town.
