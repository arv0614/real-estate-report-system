## 1. What is Tomiya City, Miyagi Prefecture: A Shining Commuter Town Near Sendai?

Tomiya City, Miyagi Prefecture, borders Sendai City—the largest metropolis in the Tohoku region—and boasts a rich history as a thriving post town on the ancient Oshu Kaido route. Since the 1970s, vast hilly areas were cleared to coincide with Sendai's development, giving birth to a succession of new towns featuring beautiful streetscapes. Through this, it rapidly grew as a commuter town for Sendai.

The greatest charm of Tomiya City lies in its "perfect sense of distance." Access to National Route 4 (国道4号線) and the Tohoku Expressway (東北自動車道), including the Izumi Interchange (泉IC), is excellent. If you commute by car, you can enjoy a smooth drive to central Sendai. While offering the convenience of hassle-free daily commutes and weekend shopping, once you step into the city, you are greeted by expansive parks and lush green landscapes.

Since transitioning from a town to a city in 2016, Tomiya has championed the slogan "Japan's No. 1 City You'll Want to Live In," driving forward its own unique urban development. As a result, it has evolved from a mere "place to sleep" into an "independent city" where working, playing, learning, and raising children can all be completely fulfilled within its borders.

## 2. The Reason Behind the Population Growth: Why It's the Top Choice for Young Families

With Japan as a whole entering an era of population decline, how does Tomiya City manage to keep growing? The biggest reason is the overwhelming support it receives from families in their 20s and 30s, who choose to move to the city when purchasing their first home.

Tomiya City is home to beautifully planned, large-scale new residential areas such as Akashidai (明石台) and Narita (成田). These areas feature spacious lot layouts, making it highly feasible to own a detached house with a garden. It’s an ideal environment for families who want to raise their children in a relaxed, open setting.

Furthermore, Tomiya consistently ranks at the top in the Tohoku region in "Best Places to Live" surveys conducted by private research institutions, proving an exceptionally high level of satisfaction among actual residents. Because so many families of the same generation live there, it’s easy to build local communities. The ability for parents to connect and for children to easily find playmates provides great peace of mind for newcomers.

This combination of tangible elements (beautiful neighborhoods and spacious homes) and intangible elements (the comfort of having many peers) is the primary reason why Tomiya City continues to be the preferred choice for young families.

## 3. An In-Depth Look at Tomiya City's Unique and Comprehensive Childcare Support

Another powerful factor drawing families to Tomiya is the extensive childcare support spearheaded by the local government. Starting in fiscal 2025, a five-year initiative called the "Tomiya City Children's Plan: Tomiya Child-Friendly City Plan" (富谷市こども計画～とみやこどもにやさしいまちプラン～) was launched, further accelerating the creation of a city where every child can grow up feeling happy.

Of particular note is the "Children and Families Center" (こども家庭センター), newly established within the city hall in April 2025. Here, resident professionals such as public health nurses and social workers provide seamless, empathetic support tailored to family concerns, from pregnancy right through the child-rearing years. For parents feeling anxious about raising a child for the first time, having accessible experts to consult with is incredibly reassuring.

Moreover, in April 2026, the "Tomiya City Child-Friendly City Ordinance" (富谷市子どもにやさしいまちづくり条例) went into effect. This is a landmark ordinance that guarantees children's rights and clearly outlines the community's commitment to watching over and nurturing children together.

Financial support is also robust. Tomiya City subsidizes medical expenses for children "until the end of the fiscal year they turn 18" (*Please check the city's latest information for details such as income limits). Since young children frequently visit the doctor for injuries or illnesses, this system—which drastically reduces the burden of medical costs—provides massive benefits to household budgets.

## 5. [For Homebuyers] The Appeal of Tomiya City as a Residential Area and How to Choose the Right Neighborhood

When you decide to buy a home in Tomiya City, what criteria should you use to select a neighborhood?

The first thing to focus on is "disaster safety." Topographically, Tomiya City is largely a hilly region, meaning most residential areas are situated on elevated ground. As a result, many areas boast a relatively low risk of flooding from river overflows—a growing concern with recent extreme weather—as well as a low risk of landslides. When choosing land while consulting a hazard map, Tomiya’s new residential neighborhoods emerge as highly viable options.

The second criterion is the "utilization of housing subsidies and support systems." Depending on the year, Miyagi Prefecture offers subsidy programs such as the "Smart Energy Housing Promotion Project" (スマートエネルギー住宅普及促進事業) for eco-friendly homebuilding, including the installation of solar panels and storage batteries (*Budgets and conditions vary by fiscal year). By smartly utilizing these systems when building a custom new home in Tomiya City, you can keep initial costs down while achieving a comfortable lifestyle with low utility bills.

As for neighborhood selection, areas like the Akashidai Higashi District (明石台東地区), which features relatively new developments and well-organized streetscapes, and the Narita District (成田地区), which offers excellent access to large commercial facilities, are particularly popular among families. It’s a good idea to narrow down the best area based on your commuting route and your children's schooling environment.

① Overview of Redevelopment
In Tomiya City, redevelopment isn’t just limited to residential areas; it is also progressing as an industrial hub. In October 2025, the large logistics facility "SANKEILOGI Sendai Izumi" (SANKEILOGI仙台泉) was completed. Furthermore, in September 2026, the city was designated by the national government as a "GX (Green Transformation) Strategic Region" (GX戦略地域). As a result, there are plans to attract large-scale data centers (DCs) and AI-related facilities to the Narita Phase 2 North Industrial Park (成田二期北工業団地).

② Impact on the Living Environment
The entry of these businesses will generate new "employment" within the city. By creating an environment where people can live near their workplaces, more residents will be able to work inside the city without commuting outside. Additionally, increased tax revenue from corporations will lead to "returns to residents," such as road improvements, park maintenance, and the expansion of childcare support. This creates a virtuous cycle that further elevates the quality of the living environment.

③ Future Asset Value (Investment Potential)
Real estate in cities with declining populations and aging infrastructure tends to drop in price. However, in a "growing city" like Tomiya—where the population is rising, new industries are emerging, and public facilities are being built—actual demand (people buying homes to live in) never ceases. Therefore, the risk of a significant collapse in land prices is considered low. It can be said that Tomiya is a highly solid area where it is easy to maintain your home's long-term asset value.
